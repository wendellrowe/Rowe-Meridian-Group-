What we collect

When you submit an inquiry, we receive only what you enter:

How it is used

Inquiry details go to the principal at hello@wendellrowe.com so we can reply, judge fit, and — if we work together — open the engagement. We do not sell this information, rent it, or share it with advertisers.

Who sees it

The principal. Cloudflare processes the form submission and hosts the site. Email routing delivers the message. No other parties receive inquiry contents unless you ask us to include them.

How long we keep it

Unanswered or declined inquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond and keep a short record of contact. Engagement files follow the life of the work. You may ask us to delete an inquiry that did not become an engagement.
